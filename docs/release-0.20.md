### Voidborn 0.20 Release Notes

## 🎉 What's New in Version 0.20

### 1. **Rally Points for Labs**
- Units created by the production queue now inherit the lab's rally orders (move, fight, patrol, guard, and more)
- Shift adds waypoints, a plain order replaces them, and stop clears them
- Every finished unit receives those orders from the lab
- The rally point carries over when the lab upgrades (T1→T2→T3)
- Forge designs follow the rally point too

### 2. **T2 Upgrade Sound**
- The upgrade gadget now plays tier2_ready.wav when a T2 upgrade finishes, and tier3_ready.wav for T3
- build_start.wav plays when an upgrade begins
- The sounds widget's T2/T3 lines have been removed to prevent them playing twice

### 3. **Weapon Graphics**
- New effect systems for all five factions:
  - Impact bursts (two sizes) with flash, glow, shockwave ring and sparks
  - Projectile trails (two sizes)
- Lasers are about 30% thicker with larger flares
- Plasma shots are about 20% bigger
- Cannons and missiles have trails
- Effect values are estimates and may need tuning in-game
- Generated by `tools/gen_fx_019.py`; safe to re-run

### 4. **Repeat on Labs**
- Labs now have the Repeat toggle (`canRepeat = true`)
- When Repeat is on, a finished queue entry goes to the back of the line with its original count
- Adding units mid-run raises the count; removing units lowers it
- The team line of forge designs never loops
- Repeat stays on after a lab upgrade
