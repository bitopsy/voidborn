THE FORGE

Voidborn is not a closed game. You can design a new unit while you play: take an existing one, change its numbers, build it a brand new 3D body and map a picture onto it. Then you pay for it like any other unit – and when it has enough experience it grows and finally splits in two.

Designing a unit

Press ~ (the key left of the 1) or type /vbforge to open the forge. The panel is draggable by its title bar and resizable by the corner handle. There is also a [+] tile at the end of the build row – click it and the forge opens right where you were. The forge is locked (the [+] tile and saved designs are greyed out) until you upgrade your factory to Tier 2.

BASE – what you build on

Pick the unit to start from. Every buildable unit is in the list with its statistics; the forge copies it and then changes what you tell it to change.

NUMBERS – the sliders

Health, metal, energy, build time, sight, speed, range, damage, reload, altitude, count and team, each with a slider, plus toggle buttons for the starting stance: fire state (return / hold / always), move state (land / air) and repeat.

IMAGE – a picture

Pick one of the pictures in unitpics/ or icons/. It is shown above the unit and can be mapped onto the 3D body.

SHAPE – a new 3D body

Build the body out of primitives: box, sphere, cylinder and cone. For each one you set the size, the position, the rotation and the colour, choose which picture is mapped onto it and which part of that picture (the UV rectangle) is used. A live 3D preview shows the result.

CREATE, SAVE, LOAD. CREATE sends the design to the game, SAVE stores it as a template and LOAD reads one back. The forge and the chat console share one template store, so a unit you build in the panel can be stamped out again with /vbspawn <id>, and a recipe you type with /vbcreate shows up in the panel.

What it costs

A unit that is stronger than the unit it was built from is not free. The forge works out the price of every custom unit automatically, relative to the cost of its base unit, so a cheap unit stays cheap and an expensive one stays expensive. The same numbers are put on the unit when it is created, and the chat log repeats them.

And build points

The premium does not only cost metal, it costs time: the unit takes (1 + 0.6 × premium / base metal) times as long to build, capped at nine times. A unit with five times the hit points and twice the damage is not built in the same time as the one it was cloned from.

A factory that is still being built cannot make anything yet. Order a unit from a hive that is under construction and it waits in the queue – the tile shows a cyan clock, the hover text says pending, and nothing is paid until the hive is finished. Then the line starts and the unit appears as usual.

Experience, growth and splitting

Every mobile unit grows as it gains experience, and when it has grown enough it splits in two. Both numbers are yours to set: the host can change them while the game runs.

0.14experience at which a unit splits in two
2×how big the unit is at that moment – it has doubled
/vbsettingsthe panel where the host changes both numbers
mod optionssplit_xp and max_growth, set in the lobby before the game

What happens at the split

  • Both halves are normal sized again, so they do not split again at once.
  • They keep the same health fraction – the damage is not doubled.
  • They keep the orders, the stances, the group and every modification the forge made (hp, speed, range, the picture, the 3D shape).
  • They start again at experience zero, and grow and split all over again.
  • Commanders and structures never split, and a team that is nearly at its unit cap does not split either.
Why split at all? A unit that has fought for a while is worth more than a fresh one, and a split gives the player two of them instead of one – at the price of the time and the metal it took to get there. Because the split copies the whole design, a unit you made in the forge splits into two of your own design.

From the console

Everything the panel does can be typed as well. The console autocompletes: press TAB to finish a command, an option name or a unit name, and the whole line is coloured while you type.

spring Voidborn 0.15.sdz Play_Choir_vs_Bot.txt

/vbcreate dread base=ch_hoplite name="Dread Lord" hp=6000 dmg=2.5 range=1.4 reload=0.6 count=3
/vbspawn dread 5            spawn more from a template
/vbtemplates   /vbforget dread   /vbbases   (list base unit names)
/vbstats       /vbforge      /vbsettings      /vbhealth      /vbranges

The free console needs cheats (/cheat) or a single human player. The forge is open to everybody in a normal match; only the lua= part of a design stays behind the cheat check.
