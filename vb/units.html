UNITS

Every army grows the same way: a commander, a factory, and two upgrades that open a deeper tier. This is the build tree of all five factions – what you build, in what order, and what each step costs.

One tree, five bodies

The tree is the same in every faction. What changes are the units, the weapons and the tricks that come with them.

Tier 1

The commander builds the structures and the first factory. The factory builds seven units: a builder, a scout, a raider, an assault unit, a long-range unit, a support unit and a heavy.

Tier 2

Upgrade the factory in place for 2000 metal and 2800 energy. It keeps every Tier 1 unit and adds five: a raider, an assault unit, an artillery unit, a support unit and a heavy. The unit forge unlocks here.

Tier 3

Upgrade again for 4500 metal and 7500 energy. The lab keeps everything below it and adds one colossus per faction: 9200 to 9800 metal, 88,000 to 120,000 HP, and about half the speed of a Tier 2 heavy.
